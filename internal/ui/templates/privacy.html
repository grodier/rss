Privacy policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

rss is a feed reader. This page says what it keeps about you, why, and who else handles it. In short: only what it needs to run your account, and nothing for ads or tracking.

Who runs rss

rss is run by [owner: your legal name, or the business that runs rss], based in [owner: country, and state if in the US]. For anything about your data, write to {{template "support-link" .SupportEmail}}.

What we keep and why

  • Your name and email address, to run your account and send you emails about it.
  • A hash of your password, never the password itself. It lets us check the password you log in with, but not recover it.
  • Your subscriptions and which articles you opened, to show your timeline and mark what you've read.
  • Your logged-in devices: a record for each, with when it was last used, so you stay logged in. It's deleted when you log out, or after 90 days without use.
  • The emails we send you, for up to 7 days after sending. Once an email is sent we keep only its address and subject: its text, with any links, is removed.

What we don't do

No ads, analytics or tracking, and we don't sell or share your data. The app loads no third-party scripts.

Who processes data for us

  • Render hosts the app and its database, in the US. Its logs of requests to the app include your IP address and are kept for about 7 days.
  • Cloudflare runs our DNS and stores the database backups.
  • Resend sends our emails, from the US.

Images in articles

Images in articles load straight from the publisher's site, which sees your IP address and browser, as it would if you visited it.

Cookies

rss sets one cookie, the session cookie, which keeps you logged in. Nothing else, so there's nothing to accept or refuse.

How long we keep data

Until you delete your account. Database backups are kept for up to 35 days, so data you delete is gone from them within 35 days.

Your choices

  • Export your subscriptions: Settings → Export subscriptions.
  • Change your name or email address in Settings.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account.
  • For anything else, such as a copy of your data, write to {{template "support-link" .SupportEmail}}.

Pre-alpha

rss is still being built. Until it's ready, its database may be wiped, accounts and all.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
